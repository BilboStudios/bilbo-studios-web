Pocket Goal support

For Pocket Goal on Android or iPhone, email info@bilbostudios.com.

Report a problem

Tell us your device model, Android or iOS version, app version, game mode and what happened. Screenshots help. Please do not send passwords, payment details or other sensitive information.

Play offline

Open Offline to play Solo, local 1 vs 1 on the same screen, or Tournament. The iOS edition from version 1.0.0 build 2 is fully offline, including Quick Match against the CPU; it has no online friend matches or invitations. Android and the legacy iOS build 1 also offer friend matches, which require an internet connection and Pocket Goal installed on both devices.

Saved progress

Progress belongs to this app installation. Uninstalling or clearing app data can erase it. There is no CrazyGames account or cloud progress sync in these mobile editions.
