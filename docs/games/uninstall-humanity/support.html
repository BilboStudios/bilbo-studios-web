Uninstall Humanity Support

For help with the Android or iOS game, email info@bilbostudios.com. Include your device model, operating-system version, game version and a description of the issue. Do not send passwords or private account information.

How to play

Tap Play, then drag anywhere to steer your car. Weapons fire automatically. Dodge and ram robots, collect experience and choose an upgrade when you level up. Use the pause button to pause, resume, restart or return to the menu.

Offline play

No internet connection or game account is required. The mobile edition has no advertising or in-app purchases. The game interface is in English.

Settings and records

Settings and personal records are stored on your device. They are not synchronized between devices. An unfinished run is not restored after closing the app. Clearing app data or deleting the app can remove your records; your operating-system backup settings may affect restoration.
