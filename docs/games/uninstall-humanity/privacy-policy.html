Uninstall Humanity — Mobile Privacy Policy

Updated October 8, 2026. This policy covers the offline Android and iOS edition of Uninstall Humanity by Bilbo Studios.

Offline gameplay and local data

The game does not require an account. Gameplay, graphics, sound and fonts are included in the app. Settings and personal gameplay records are stored locally on your device. We do not collect or transmit this information to our servers. The game does not synchronize progress between devices.

No advertising or tracking

This mobile edition has no advertising, in-app purchases, external analytics or tracking SDKs. The game does not collect personal data or share it with third parties. It does not request access to your location, contacts, camera or microphone.

Your choices

You can remove local game data by clearing the app data or deleting the app. Device or operating-system backup settings may affect whether data is restored when you reinstall. No account deletion process is needed because the game has no accounts.

Support and platform services

If you contact us by email, we receive the address and information you choose to send and use them to respond to your request. Apple and Google process store-related information and may process diagnostic or backup information under their own policies and your device settings. This policy describes the game itself.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, email info@bilbostudios.com. Do not send passwords or other unnecessary private information.
