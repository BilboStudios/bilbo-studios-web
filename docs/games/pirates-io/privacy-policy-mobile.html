Pirates.io — Mobile Privacy Policy

Updated October 4, 2026. This policy applies to the offline Android and iOS edition published by Bilbo Studios.

Offline game

You play against computer-controlled ships. The app does not connect to our multiplayer servers and does not include advertising, in-app purchases, portal SDKs or analytics providers.

Data stored on your device

Your captain name, randomly generated local profile identifier, gold, gems, cosmetics, achievements, quests, daily rewards and preferences are stored on your device. The local identifier is used to associate your saved progress with your profile. This data is not transmitted to Bilbo Studios or other players. Operating-system backups may retain app data according to your device and account settings.

Deleting your progress

You can delete the app's stored data through Android Settings or delete the app on iOS. Uninstalling may remove your progress. There is no online account or cloud synchronization managed by Bilbo Studios.

Support and platform services

If you email us, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to respond. Apple and Google may process store, purchase, diagnostic or backup information under their own policies and your settings. The game does not request access to your contacts, location, camera or microphone.

Contact

For privacy or support requests: info@bilbostudios.com.
