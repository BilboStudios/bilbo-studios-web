Stellar Swarm for iOS — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Overview

This policy covers the iOS app Stellar Swarm (com.bilbostudios.stellarswarm), published under the Bilbo Studios brand. It is a single-player game that works offline. Web versions distributed by game portals have separate services and privacy notices.

Data Collection

The iOS game does not collect or transmit personal data to Bilbo Studios. No account, registration, or personal information is required to play. The app does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, or microphone.

Local Storage

The game saves progress, unlocked content, achievements, records, and settings in local app storage on your device. Bilbo Studios does not receive this information or provide a cloud-save service. iOS may back up or transfer app data according to your device settings and Apple's policies. Deleting the app removes its local progress; restoring a device backup may restore app data.

Third-Party Services

This iOS release has no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. Fonts and game resources are bundled with the app. Apple may independently process store, installation, backup, or diagnostic information under its own policies; this is separate from data collected by the game. TestFlight beta testing may provide crash reports, usage information, and feedback through Apple's testing service.

Optional Sharing and Contact

If you choose to export or share a run card, it contains your game results and is sent only through the destination you select. Sharing services handle that content under their own policies. If you email us for support, we receive your email address and the information you include, and use it to respond to your request. We do not use support messages for advertising.

In-App Purchases

This iOS release has no in-app purchases and does not process payment information.

Children's Privacy

The game does not collect personal information from children or other players. Its store age rating is determined through Apple's App Store content-rating process.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

Contact

For privacy or support requests, contact Bilbo Studios at admin@bilbostudios.com. To request removal of a support email, contact us from the same address. We retain support correspondence only as needed to resolve the request and comply with applicable obligations.
